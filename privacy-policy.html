Tallyheart Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026 · Terms of Use

Tallyheart is a private sexual-health journal published by Zero Day Gaming ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short, it doesn't leave your phone.

  • Your data stays on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • We collect nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • We don't share, sell or use your data in any way, because we never have it.

Information stored by the app

Everything you enter, including your name and gender, partners, encounters, notes, tags, test records and attachments, is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Test attachments and exported backups can be encrypted by the app (AES-256), with keys kept in your device's secure storage.

Information we collect

None. Tallyheart has no user accounts and no servers that receive your records. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software. We cannot see your data and could not provide it to anyone, because we do not have it.

When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to send it:

  • Backups you export. The file is saved wherever you choose, and you control it from then on.
  • Reports you share. Partner or clinic reports go only to the app or person you pick in your phone's share sheet.

App data is excluded from Android and iOS device and cloud backups.

Internet use and purchases

The app connects to the internet only to buy, check or restore the optional premium upgrade through Google Play or the Apple App Store. That exchange is handled by the store under its own privacy policy. None of your Tallyheart records are sent. Testing reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Device permissions

  • Notifications: to show testing reminders you turn on.
  • Biometrics: to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face, if you enable app lock. Biometric data never leaves your device's secure hardware and is not available to the app.
  • Photos and files: only when you pick a test result or backup to attach or import.
  • Network: only for store purchases, as described above.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all data in the app, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, deleted data cannot be recovered.

Children

Tallyheart is intended for adults 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

Tallyheart is a support tool. It does not provide medical advice and is not a definitive source on whether to inform a partner. See the Terms of Use for details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above, and the app will ask you to review significant changes.

Contact

Questions: tallyheartapp@gmail.com. Please don't include personal health information in your message. We use emails only to reply to you.
